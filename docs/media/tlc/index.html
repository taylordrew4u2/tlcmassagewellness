The portfolio story

A welcoming site.
An owner who can keep it current.

The brief

Create a website for a massage and wellness practice that introduces the treatments, guides visitors into an appointment request and gives the owner control over day-to-day content.

The design

Forest green, antique gold and warm cream carry the butterfly identity through both the public site and admin. Cormorant Garamond headings and Jost supporting text create a calm, readable rhythm.

#2f3b2a#a89240#f7f3e8

The functionality

  • Treatment browsing and appointment requests
  • Owner review: accept, decline and complete
  • Editable content, treatments, images and profiles
  • Optional custom intake questions
  • Solo practitioner and team modes

The build

Next.js, React, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS, with Postgres storage and Vercel Blob upload support.

Appointments require an owner response. Prepared replies open the owner's email app. Persistent storage and photo uploads require their respective service configuration.
