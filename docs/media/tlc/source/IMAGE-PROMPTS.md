# Image prompts and provenance

The following two images were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. They are conceptual brand photography for TLC Massage Wellness. They do not show the actual premises, practitioner, products or services of a verified operating practice. No supplied photograph was used as a reference.

The finished promotional layouts combine these conceptual images with separate interface captures and professionally typeset copy. The interface captures come from the local project demo with sample content. See `../USAGE.md` for publication notes.

## `images/wellness-room.png`

Original generated dimensions: **1672 × 941 pixels**. The source is preserved without retouching or resizing. This landscape image provides room for headlines on the left.

Exact prompt:

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: README and portfolio brand atmosphere photograph for TLC Massage Wellness, conceptual brand imagery rather than a photograph of an actual practice.
Primary request: Serene private massage treatment room, forest green linen on treatment table, warm cream plaster walls, antique gold accents, small ceramic bowl and one folded towel, subtle leafy branch shadows from a daylight window.
Style/medium: Tactile unretouched editorial architectural photography, calm professional welcoming and realistic rather than a luxury resort.
Composition/framing: Wide 16:9 composition, ideally 1920x1080 pixels. Treatment table in right half, with generous quiet room on the left for later typeset headlines.
Lighting/mood: Soft natural daylight; quiet, grounded, restorative and trustworthy.
Color palette: Forest green #2f3b2a, antique gold #a89240, cream #f7f3e8.
Constraints: No people, no text, no logos, no watermark. No products, oil labels, candles, or spa cliches.
```

## `images/wellness-detail.png`

Portrait source for social and portfolio layouts. Its upper negative space allows copy to be typeset separately.

Exact prompt:

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: portrait editorial brand photograph for TLC Massage Wellness social campaign and portfolio, conceptual brand imagery rather than a real practice photograph.
Primary request: Create a refined, realistic wellness still-life with textured forest-green linen, neatly rolled warm cream towels, a matte cream ceramic dish, and one subtle fresh botanical branch.
Composition/framing: Portrait 4:5 composition. Close-up detail with the arrangement mostly in the lower half. Leave softly lit cream and green negative space in the top half for later professionally typeset copy. Restrained, balanced composition, premium editorial photography.
Lighting/mood: Quiet natural window lighting with antique gold sunlight, gentle shadows, serene and grounded.
Color palette: Forest green #2f3b2a, antique gold #a89240, warm cream #f7f3e8.
Materials/textures: Beautifully realistic woven linen, plush cotton towel fibers, matte ceramic, fresh botanical texture.
Constraints: No text, no logo, no watermark, no people, no medical objects, no bottles, no claims.
```
